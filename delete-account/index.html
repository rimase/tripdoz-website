OPTION 01

Delete your account in the app

To delete your TRIPDOZ account:

  1. Open TRIPDOZ and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

After deletion, you will be signed out and will no longer be able to access that account.

OPTION 02

Can't access the app?

You can request deletion without signing in. Email tripdozapp@gmail.com with the subject TRIPDOZ Account Deletion Request.

Where possible, send the request from the email address registered to your TRIPDOZ account. If you cannot access that email, explain this in your message. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before processing the request.

YOUR DATA

What is deleted or retained?

Account deletion removes your TRIPDOZ sign-in account and associated personal records, such as your profile information, saved places, and linked reviews or contributions, from the active app database.

Uploaded image files, technical logs, backups, and records that must be retained for security or legal reasons may be subject to separate deletion processes or limited retention. Where data must be retained, we limit it to the applicable purpose and retention requirements.

If you want confirmation about a particular uploaded photo or other data, include that detail in your deletion request. We will review it and explain any applicable retention or exceptions.
