Section 01

Introduction & Overview

Welcome to TRIPDOZ (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We believe meaningful exploration requires trust, clarity, and unobtrusive digital tools. This Privacy Policy explains how we gather, utilize, and protect your information when you access the TRIPDOZ website and mobile experience.

Browsing without compulsory registration
You can explore publicly available destination information without creating an account. Some personalized or community features may require sign-in.
Section 02

Information We Collect

We aim to collect only information necessary to provide and improve the service.

Technical Device Data
Basic device, browser and diagnostic information may be processed by service providers.
Voluntary Feedback
Messages, corrections and suggestions you choose to submit.
Photo Contributions
Images and related information you voluntarily upload.

When you create an account, we may process account identifiers, authentication information, saved content and reviews.

Section 03

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the app, maintain destination information, enable saved places and community contributions, respond to support requests, and protect service integrity.

Curated Discovery
Improve destination browsing, helpful information and user experience.
Community Safety
Review submitted content, prevent misuse, and maintain accurate listings.
Section 04

Location Data & Permissions

Location permissions, when requested, should be optional and used only for the feature explained at the time of the request. You may disable location access through your device settings.

No continuous tracking intended
TRIPDOZ is not designed as a background movement tracker.
Nearby Discovery
Location can support nearby recommendations when enabled.
Manual Selection
Browse governorates and cities without sharing precise location.
Section 05

TRIPDOZ may use external infrastructure, authentication, mapping, and hosting providers. Their processing is governed by their applicable policies. Links to external websites operate under their own terms.

Section 06

Data Security & Storage

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Data may be stored with trusted cloud providers supporting the app.

Section 07

Your Rights & Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and operational requirements. You can revoke optional device permissions in your settings.

Access

Deletion

Permissions
Manage camera, photo and location access on your device.
